Color Snake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Color Snake ("the app") is a single-player game with an optional global leaderboard. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

What the app collects

  • An anonymous account identifier. On first launch, the app creates an anonymous account for your device (via Supabase) with no email, password, or sign-in screen involved. This identifier is only used to tell your leaderboard entry apart from everyone else's — it isn't linked to your name, email, or any other personal information.
  • A display name you choose. If you enter a name in Settings, it's shown publicly next to your score on the global leaderboard. Don't use your real name or any other personal information as your display name if you'd rather it stay private.
  • Your best score, level, and survival time, submitted to the global leaderboard whenever you beat your own previous best.

What stays on your device

Your gold balance, owned/equipped skins, sound and music settings, daily spin streak, and personal best stats are all stored locally on your device and are never sent anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, or camera
  • No sharing or selling of any data to third parties

Children's privacy

The app doesn't knowingly collect any personal information — the only data tied to an account is an anonymous identifier and a display name you choose yourself, and that applies equally to players of any age.

Data deletion

To request that your leaderboard entry be deleted, contact us at the email below. Since accounts are anonymous, please mention your display name and approximate score so we can find the right entry.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change too.
