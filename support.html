Common questions

My progress/gold disappeared after reinstalling the app.
Gold, unlocked skins, and your personal best stats are all stored on your device only, so they don't carry over to a new device or survive an uninstall. Your position on the global leaderboard is tied to your account and isn't affected by this.

How do I change my leaderboard display name?
Open Settings from the main menu — there's a Display Name field there.

I want my leaderboard entry removed.
Email the address above with your display name and approximate score so we can find the right entry.

Does the game cost anything / show ads?
No — Color Snake Classic is free to play with no ads.
